# WellOne Customer v77

Customer storefront update for live product availability and promotional offers.

## Included
- Live stock availability for products and individual variants.
- Automatic Out of Stock handling when inventory reaches zero.
- Cart quantity limits based on current availability.
- Availability is checked again before WhatsApp checkout.
- Separate Offer Items section while preserving existing promotional banners.
- Responsive and mobile-optimized customer experience.
- Standard customer product search only.

Inventory management and product identification remain within the admin system.
